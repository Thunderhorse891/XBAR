New price disclosure: “Prices in USD, billed monthly. Annual billing is not offered yet.” Each card adds “Up to [plan limit] horses” using the existing canonical plan limit. Existing plan names, monthly prices, limits, and included features are unchanged.

The old FAQ (local/cloud setup, changing plans, ranch size, checkout) is replaced by these four questions and answers, also used for structured search data:

- **Can I cancel anytime?** Manage your subscription through Stripe from Billing in XBAR. Stripe shows the cancellation options and the date your paid access ends. If those controls are unavailable, contact XBAR support before buying another plan.
- **What happens to my records if I cancel?** Canceling a plan does not delete your ranch records. When paid access ends, higher-plan tools and limits are no longer available. Export important records before changing your plan. Deleting your account is a separate action.
- **Do you offer annual billing?** Only monthly billing is offered here. Annual prices and discounts have not been set.
- **How do I choose or change a plan?** Choose a plan below, sign in or create your account, and review it in Billing. A new subscription uses secure Stripe checkout. To change an existing subscription, use Manage billing so you do not start a second subscription.
